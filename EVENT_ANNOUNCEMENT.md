# Live Coding Event Announcement

Join us for a live coding exercise with a twist: the builder doesn't write a single line of code. Designed for anyone curious about using Google Antigravity for knowledge work, this session shows how a complete physical product (a factory-ready modular synth module) and its companion website came together in a couple of days from a hand-drawn circuit sketch. Under the hood, Antigravity writes and runs plenty of code to analyze the design, lay out the circuit board, generate custom front-panel artwork, verify manufacturing files, and build the site. The human stays focused on direction, trade-offs, and decisions. Bring a laptop to follow along hands-on, or simply sit back and watch the process unfold live.

- **Tutorial:** https://domesticmouse.github.io/EnvelopeFollower/
- **Product:** https://domesticmouse.github.io/EnvelopeFollowerWithSkewLimiter/
